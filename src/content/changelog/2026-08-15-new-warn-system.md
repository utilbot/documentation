We've rebuilt the bot's alert system to offer more features in the long term. Previously, alerts were simply counted as a counter per user, without saving any additional context (date, time, sender).

Effective immediately, this data is now stored along with warnings; the other features remain unaffected for the time being. You can also now view your own warnings and their status in the Utilbot Dashboard.

Migrated warnings are now dated as of the migration date; the system is listed as the issuer, and the migration is specified as the reason for the warning.
